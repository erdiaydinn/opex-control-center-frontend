OPEX Control Center Inventory V25
Release date: 2026-07-29

ACCESS SCOPE SEPARATION
- The Control Center "Access Control" card now opens platform user, group and module permissions.
- Inventory counter accounts and warehouse/server-group assignments moved under Inventory administration.
- Inventory accounts are explicitly labelled as Inventory-only and do not grant OPEX platform access.
- The legacy /admin/accounts path redirects to /inventory/access-management.
- Only users with Inventory admin permission can open the Inventory account management page.
