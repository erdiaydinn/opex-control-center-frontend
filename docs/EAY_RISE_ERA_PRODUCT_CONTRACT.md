# EAY Rise Era Product Contract

Status: canonical development direction for the category-leadership continuation.

## Product rule
EAY modules must be independently useful and commercially viable. Cross-module installation may add intelligence, but no commercial module may require an unrelated module merely to provide its core value.

## Non-negotiable platform invariants
- central server-authoritative identity and authorization
- tenant isolation and RLS where persistence applies
- fail-closed security and no browser-derived authority
- immutable/auditable sensitive actions
- privacy and data minimization
- platform localization contract: TR, EN, DE, AR, FR, ES, IT, NL, PL, PT-BR; Arabic RTL
- accessibility and explicit loading/error/empty/offline states
- synthetic/repository evidence must never be presented as field/production evidence

## Category leadership tracks
1. Production truth and external acceptance remain P0.
2. Workforce evolves from demand calculation to demand/capacity/KPI/pressure/root-cause/replanning intelligence while preserving customer rule configuration.
3. Field Intelligence becomes a standalone physical-truth product: smart target selection, live missions, evidence, reminders, rework, escalation and verified results.
4. Business Glossary becomes tenant-aware semantic authority for Jarvis, KPI surfaces, Academy and product help.
5. Audit is a planned standalone module. Its question model and detailed workflow are intentionally deferred until product discovery is completed; shared identity, evidence, targeting, localization, reminder/escalation and immutable audit primitives should be reusable by it.

## Customer-specific semantics
User-supplied operational examples are reference implementations, not EAY-global constants. KPI definitions, terminology, formulas, policies and workforce rules must be scoped and versioned for the customer/tenant and, where needed, country/region/business unit.

Resolution order for business semantics should be explicit and deterministic, for example tenant/business-unit/country overrides over approved EAY reference concepts. Undefined company concepts must not be fabricated by Jarvis.

## Development gate
A category-leadership feature is admitted when it closes a verified competitor/customer gap, increases production acceptance, or creates defensible cross-module operational intelligence. It must not weaken the platform invariants above.
